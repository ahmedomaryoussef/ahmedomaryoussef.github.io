01 About

Ahmed Omar Youssef is an Assistant Professor of Private Law at Kingdom University, Bahrain, and Lecturer (Assistant Professor) of Private International Law at Capital University, Egypt.

He is also Co-Founder of Al Nokhba Group for Law and Legal Consultations, where he practises as a lawyer specialising in international contracting, cross-border disputes, international jurisdiction, nationality and the status of foreigners, and private international law matters generally, with practical experience in the Arabian Gulf region. This practice informs his scholarship and keeps his teaching close to how cross-border legal questions arise in real cases.
